# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Individuals managing personal finances locally who need to review transactions, budgets, categories, trends, and financial health with confidence and minimal friction.

## Product Purpose

MoneyPot is a private, local-first expense tracker that makes everyday financial activity understandable and actionable. Success means users can enter, import, inspect, and correct financial data quickly, then understand their current position without decoding the interface.

## Brand Personality

Professional, calm, precise. The product should feel dependable and focused, with a quiet sense of control appropriate for sensitive financial information.

## Anti-references

Avoid playful consumer-fintech decoration, promotional dashboard theatrics, excessive gradients or glow, oversized metrics without context, inconsistent controls, and dense enterprise accounting software that obscures common tasks.

## Design Principles

- Make financial status legible at a glance while preserving access to detail.
- Use one familiar interaction vocabulary across every workflow.
- Keep primary actions obvious and destructive actions deliberate.
- Let data carry visual emphasis; interface chrome remains restrained.
- Design every state, including loading, empty, error, disabled, hover, and keyboard focus.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient text and control contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic labels, and redundant status cues that do not depend on color alone.
